--- NIMS Doctrine Excerpt ---
The National Incident Management System (NIMS) provides a systematic,
proactive approach to guide all levels of government, nongovernmental
organizations, and the private sector to work together to prevent,
protect against, mitigate, respond to, and recover from the effects
of incidents.

--- ICS Forms Reference ---
ICS-209: Incident Status Summary. Used for reporting incident status.
ICS-204: Assignment List. Used for division/group assignment.
ICS-201: Incident Briefing. First document completed at the start of an incident.
